OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy First.

We designed PaySplitter with zero ad trackers, no data brokers, and full transparent control over your financial records.

Last updated: August 2026 • v5.21 Compliant
Section 01

Information We Collect

We adhere to data minimization — we only collect what is strictly necessary to run real-time expense calculations and device synchronization.

Profile Credentials

Your name, verified email address, and optional custom profile avatar photo uploaded during or after account creation.

Shared Trip Data

Details regarding your trips, shared expense entries, payer/borrower splits, settlement receipts, and digital pot balances.

Feedback & Communication

Voluntary star ratings, suggestion notes, and troubleshooting feedback submitted through our in-app feedback channel.

Section 02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly processed to power the mathematical algorithms and cross-device sync of the PaySplitter application:

  • Instant Multi-Device Sync: Automatically keeps your trip balances, debt calculations, and expenses in sync across Android and Web.
  • Group Collaboration: Securely shares expense records only with members you explicitly invite to your private trips.
  • Account Security: Safeguards access to your account with cryptographic session tokens and verification checks.

Zero Commercial Exploitation: We do not sell, rent, monetize, track, or share your financial data with any third-party advertising networks.

Section 03

Data Deletion & Your Rights

In full accordance with GDPR and Google Play Developer Policies, you possess the absolute Right to be Forgotten. You can permanently erase your entire profile and login credentials directly from inside the app.

In-App Account Deletion Guide
1
Open PaySplitter and tap your Profile Avatar in the top-right header.
2
Select About PaySplitter from the menu drawer.
3
Tap the Privacy Policy button in the footer.
4
Scroll down and tap the red Delete Account button.
5
Type DELETE in capital letters to confirm permanent erasure.
Immediate Effect: Your account records, credentials, and profile entries are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.
Section 04

Infrastructure & Security

We rely strictly on battle-tested, enterprise-grade cloud providers with automated encryption protocols.

F

Google Firebase Cloud Platform

Provides cloud database replication and authentication. All network transfers use HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption, and database volumes are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

Fs

Formspree Relay

Securely processes voluntary suggestions and ratings submitted through the in-app feedback dialog without tracking cookies.
