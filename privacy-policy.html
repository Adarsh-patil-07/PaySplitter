01

Information we collect

When you use PaySplitter, we only collect what's needed to run the app well — nothing more.

Profile information

Name, email address, and an optional custom avatar you upload during or after sign-up.

App data

Trips, groups, shared expenses, settlements, custom payment details, and digital pot balances.

Feedback & communications

Voluntary ratings, suggestions, and messages you submit through our in-app feedback system.

02

How we use your data

The data we collect is strictly used to provide the core functionality and reliability of the app:

  • Sync your expenses and balances seamlessly across all your devices, in real time.
  • Share expense details securely with the specific users you invite to your trips or groups.
  • Provide secure authentication and protect your account from unauthorized access.
  • Review feedback to improve app features, performance, and stability.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

03

Data deletion & your rights

You have full control over your personal data — including the right to permanently delete your account and everything tied to it, at any time. This is your Right to be Forgotten.

How to delete your data — in the app
1

Tap your Profile avatar in the top header

2

Tap About PaySplitter

3

Tap Privacy Policy

4

Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account

5

Type DELETE to confirm

Your account, profile records, and login credentials are erased immediately. This cannot be undone.
