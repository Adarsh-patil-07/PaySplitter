The Official User Guide

Group finances, simplified.

PaySplitter is built for travel, roommates, and friends. Enjoy 100% offline functionality, private on-device storage, zero mandatory logins, and instant smart settlements.

01

Home Dashboard

The central command center for all your journeys. View your overall balance, active trips, and quick tools at a glance.

  • Real-Time Net Balance Overview

    Instantly see your overall standing across all journeys. Your balance clearly separates Payable (money you owe to others) from Receivable (money others owe you), keeping your personal finances transparent.

  • Active Trip Workspaces & Live Ticker

    Each card represents an ongoing or past trip, showing total group spending, participant avatars, and an activity ticker that updates immediately whenever a new expense or settlement occurs.

  • Quick Action Dock

    The bottom navigation bar gives you one-tap shortcuts to your trips list, travel route map, in-trip group chat, member roster, archive, and the floating button to create or join a trip.

Fig 1.1 Main Home Dashboard Overview
02

Trip Setup & Customization

Create a trip in seconds with custom themes, travel icons, date ranges, and zero-friction member adding.

Fig 2.1 Create New Trip Modal
  • Personalized Themes & Travel Icons

    Give your trip a custom identity. Choose from diverse travel icons (flights, road trips, mountains, beaches) and select vibrant gradient materials to style your trip card.

  • Flexible Dates & Budget Tracking

    Set optional start and end dates to keep expenses organized chronologically. Define a trip budget target to help your group stay on track with spending goals.

  • Instant Member Entry & Digital Pot

    Add friends simply by typing their names—no phone numbers, passwords, or invitations needed. Toggle on the Digital Pot if your group prefers pooling money upfront.

🛡️
Zero Signups Required: Friends are added immediately without any onboarding friction.
03

Smart Expense Splitting

Record bills effortlessly with intelligent categorization, flexible payer assignments, and exclusion controls.

  • Quick Bill Logging & Categories

    Enter the bill description, amount, and category (Food, Travel, Stay, Activities). Categories automatically power your visual spending analytics and summary reports.

  • Custom Payers & Selective Exclusions

    Assign who fronted the money—an individual member or the shared Digital Pot. Use the exclusion filter to easily omit members who did not participate in a specific meal or activity.

  • Interactive Day Strip & Notes

    Tap through the horizontal calendar strip to assign expenses to specific trip days, and add notes to record invoice details, receipt notes, or vendor info.

Fig 3.1 Add Expense Screen
04

Balances & Simplified Debts

Eliminate confusing round-robin debts. View direct settlement paths and the itemized member ledger.

Fig 4.1 Net Balances & Debt Sheet
  • "Who Owes Whom" Smart Settlements

    Our debt simplification algorithm mathematically minimizes crisscrossed debts into the smallest possible number of direct transfers, saving everyone from endless back-and-forth payments.

  • Individual Contribution Ledger

    A transparent breakdown detailing what each person spent versus their fair share. Positive balances highlight members who are owed money, while negative balances show amounts to be settled.

⚡
Mathematically Optimal: Collapses dozens of chaotic group expenses into minimal direct payments.

Settle Up (Pay Now & Paid Status)

Fig 5.1 Pay Now: Instant UPI Launch

Fig 5.2 Paid Status: Verification & History

  • Audit Transparency & Accountability

    Provides a permanent record of past actions, preventing misunderstandings and ensuring total team trust throughout the journey.

Visual breakdowns of categories, top spenders, largest items, and individual itemized costs.

08

The Digital Pot (Kitty Fund)

Pool group funds upfront, track live kitty balances, and let the equalization engine settle deposit differences.

  • Shared Group Kitty & Live Meter

    Collect equal upfront funds into a digital pot before traveling. Monitor total funded amounts versus spent balances in real time, and top up whenever needed.

  • Smart Pot Equalization Engine

    If members deposit unequal amounts into the pot, the built-in Equalization calculator automatically determines the exact leveling transfers to keep everyone's contribution fair.

  • Frictionless Communal Spending

    Pay for fuel, tolls, snacks, and shared meals directly from the Pot without calculating per-person micro-splits on the go.

🍯
Traveler Favorite: Eliminates 80% of small payment friction during road trips and group vacations.
Fig 8.1 Digital Pot Kitty View
09

Map Route & Travel Itinerary

Visualize waypoints, checkpoints, total distances, and driving times on a GPS satellite route map.

Fig 9.1 Map Itinerary & Route Corridor
  • Driving Distance & Time Estimates

    The top route overview displays total planned stops, total driving distance, and estimated travel duration across your journey.

  • Numbered Checkpoint Timeline

    Color-coded waypoints map your complete travel route from departure to final destination, showing distances and driving times between each stop.

  • Route Controls & Navigation

    Easily edit stops, toggle return routes for round trips, or launch active navigation in your preferred maps application.

10

In-Trip Chat & Notes

An integrated group channel with pinned cloud folders, location pins, and shared notes.

  • Pinned Cloud Drive Folders

    Pin essential group assets—such as shared Google Drive folders for trip photos, tickets, and bookings—directly to the top of the chat for instant access.

  • Location Pins & Group Messaging

    Share clickable Google Maps location pins, packing checklists, and announcements without cluttering personal messaging apps.

  • Context-Locked Discussions

    Every message and link stays attached to the specific trip, making it effortless to retrieve past memories and recommendations.

12

App Lock & Local Privacy

Bank-grade security keeping your personal financial records and balances protected on-device.

  • Custom 4-Digit PIN Security

    Lock your app behind a custom 4-digit PIN using an intuitive dark touch dialpad, safeguarding sensitive balance sheets from curious eyes.

  • 100% Offline Local Sovereignty

    All expense calculations, balances, and chat notes are stored strictly on your local device—ensuring complete privacy with zero external tracking.
