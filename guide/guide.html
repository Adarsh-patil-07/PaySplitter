The Official User Guide

Group finances, simplified.

PaySplitter is built for travel, roommates, and friends. Enjoy 100% offline functionality, private on-device storage, zero mandatory logins, and instant smart settlements.

01

Home Dashboard

The central command center for all your journeys. View your overall balance, active trips, and quick tools at a glance.

  • 1
    Total Balance & Net Standing Banner

    Displays your combined trip balance (e.g. ₹4,797.00) divided into real-time PAYABLE (e.g. ₹258.67 with red up-arrow) and RECEIVABLE (e.g. ₹0.00 with green down-arrow) summaries.

  • 2
    Active Trip Cards & Live Activity

    Cards highlight the trip name (e.g. Netravati), total expenditure (₹4,797), participant avatars, and an activity ticker showing recent actions like "New expense in a trip: ₹800.00 • 9 Sept".

  • 3
    Quick Action Dock

    Bottom navigation bar provides one-tap access to Trips, Route Map (📍), Trip Chat (💬), Trash / Archive (🗑️), Members (👥), and the floating ➕ button to create or join trips.

02

Trip Setup & Customization

Create a trip in seconds with custom themes, travel icons, date ranges, and zero-friction member adding.

Fig 2.1 Create New Trip Modal with Themes & Digital Pot
  • 1
    Title, Icons & Card Material Swatches

    Enter your Trip Title (e.g. "Goa Trip"), select from 8 travel icons (Flight ✈️, Car 🚗, Scooter 🛵, Beach 🏖️, Mountain ⛰️, Camping 🏕️, Monument 🏛️, Cruise 🛳️), and choose from 8 gradient Card Material swatches (Purple, Navy, Emerald, Coral, Sky Blue, Teal, Violet, Slate).

  • 2
    Date Pickers & Budget Target

    Set optional Start Date and End Date ranges, along with an optional Trip Budget target (e.g. ₹50,000) to track team spending thresholds.

  • 3
    Instant Members (Min 2, Max 10) & Digital Pot Toggle

    Type member names directly without phone numbers or passwords. Check "Enable Digital Pot (Prepaid Fund)" if your group wants an upfront pooled kitty balance.

🛡️
Zero Signups Required: Add up to 10 friends instantly. No logins, accounts, or invitations required to start calculating.
03

Smart Expense Splitting

Log bills with automated categorization, custom payers, exclusion controls, and an interactive day picker.

  • 1
    Description, Amount & Category

    Type "What was it for?" (e.g. Petrol, Dinner, Entry Tickets), input the Amount (₹), and choose a category from the dropdown (such as Food, Travel, Stay, or Activities).

  • 2
    Paid By & Split Exclusions

    Select who paid from the "Paid By" dropdown (e.g. Adarsh Patil). Use the "Exclude someone" dropdown to split equally among everyone or selectively exclude non-participating members.

  • 3
    Horizontal Date Strip & Notes

    Quickly tap the exact date on the interactive calendar strip (e.g. MON 7, TUE 8, WED 9 active cyan pill, THU 10, FRI 11) and add optional notes before hitting "Save Expense".

08

The Digital Pot (Kitty Fund)

Pool group funds upfront, track live kitty balances, and let the equalization engine settle deposit differences.

  • 1
    Live Pot Balance & Top-Up Meter

    Displays total pooled funds (e.g. ₹3,500.00) alongside a real-time status strip: Funded: ₹3,500.00 | Spent: ₹0.00. Tap + Top Up Pot anytime to add more funds.

  • 2
    Smart "Pot Equalization" Engine

    When members contribute unequal deposits, the POT EQUALIZATION card calculates exact balance-leveling transfers (e.g. Adarsh Patil ➔ Chaitra Naik: ₹166.67 and Vishal Patil ➔ Chaitra Naik: ₹166.67) so everyone's net contribution is 100% equal.

  • 3
    Zero-Friction Group Bills

    When paying for fuel, tolls, or snacks, simply pick "Digital Pot" as the payer—eliminating messy per-person split arithmetic on the road.

🍯
Group Favorite: Cuts out 80% of tiny individual transaction friction during road trips.
